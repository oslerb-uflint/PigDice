Here, I will add some commentary about my development process.

Misc Assignment 1 stage: I wanted to try playing around with making the random number generation less resource intensive and use less lines of code. 
In this version I created the generator and distribution objects and assigned them to member variables in the Die class. This way, there wouldn't need
to be a new generator and new distribution created every time a random number is needed. I create a new distribution whenever the number of sides of 
the die is changed.
